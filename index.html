Getting started

  1. Duplicate your current theme before you change themes. Customizations made to another theme do not carry over.
  2. In Online Store > Themes, add Tahvia and select Customize.
  3. Pick your hero product in the Dial hero section on the home page.
  4. Upload product images with a transparent background (.png, 2000 x 2000px recommended). Tahvia places them on the dial, so a cut-out object looks best.
  5. In Theme settings > Colors, pick the dial color scheme. Every section with the dial background uses it.

Header and footer

The header group holds the announcement bar and the header (main menu with full-width submenus, search, account and cart). The footer group holds the footer: menus, text, email signup, app blocks, country and language selectors, payment icons, Follow on Shop and policy links.

Page templates

  • page: title and side menu in a sticky column, text on the right, a help card at the end. Used for shipping, warranty and similar pages.
  • page.about: a story page made of sections.
  • page.contact: contact details on the left, the form on the right.

Product pages

The product information section is made of blocks: vendor, title, price, SKU, inventory status, variant picker, quantity selector, buy buttons, description, collapsible rows, share, complementary products, text, Custom Liquid and app blocks.

  • Variant images: the gallery shows the image assigned to the selected variant.
  • Swatches: set a color or image on option values in the product admin.
  • Unit prices, compare-at prices, sale and sold out states update with the variant.
  • Dynamic checkout buttons (on by default) and the Shop Pay Installments banner.
  • Pickup availability, related and complementary products (Search and Discovery app).
  • Gift card recipient form.
  • Images, videos, YouTube or Vimeo videos and 3D models with "View in your space".
  • Selling plans (subscriptions) when an app provides them.

To tell the full story on the product page, create a product template that adds Story chapters, Spec sheet and Exploded view under the product information, then assign it to your hero product in the product admin (Theme template).

The dial light

The background of the dial sections is a sunray dial: fine radial brushing with a band of light that turns with the customer's real seconds, scroll position and pointer.

  • It starts after the page has loaded, so it never slows the first paint.
  • It pauses when no dial section is on screen or when the tab is hidden.
  • Without WebGL, with Save-Data, with reduced motion, on devices without a graphics card, or with Show animated dial light turned off, customers see a still brushed dial drawn with CSS.

Accessibility and speed

  • Skip link, visible focus on every control, keyboard-operable menus, drawers and dialogs. Escape closes them and focus returns to the control that opened them.
  • Every image has alt text from the product admin or the image settings.
  • Text contrast meets WCAG AA in the four default color schemes. Check contrast when you change colors.
  • No third-party libraries. JavaScript is split into modules that load only on pages that use them.

FAQ

Can I use Tahvia for a store with many products?

Yes. Collection, search and cart pages are complete. The home page is designed around one product.

My product image has a white background.

It works, but the dial shows around it as a square. Remove the background in the Shopify admin image editor or upload a .png with transparency.

How do I link the hero button to the buy box on the home page?

Set the featured product section's anchor ID (default buy) and use #buy as the button link.

How do I turn off all motion?

Turn off the four settings in Theme settings > Animations.

Where do the policy pages get their text?

From Settings > Policies in your Shopify admin. Tahvia styles them with the same layout as your help pages.

Can you customize the theme for me?

Custom code is not covered by support. Duplicate your theme before any change, and hire a Shopify Partner for custom work.
